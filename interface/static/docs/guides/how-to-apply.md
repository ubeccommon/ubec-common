# How to Apply for UBEC Support

The UBEC Protocol funds regenerative food systems and local food sovereignty.
Sixty-five percent of the token supply is dedicated to supporting small farms
and communities committed to regenerative agriculture — this is the heart of
what the commons exists to do.

This guide walks you through applying. Whichever pathway fits you, the process
is the same: choose your pathway, complete the form, and receive a reference
number you can use to follow up.

## Choosing your pathway

There are four pathways, each supporting a different role in the bioregional
commons:

- **Farmer** — for individual farmers and growers committed to regenerative
  agriculture, seeking support to transition and sustain practices that heal
  the land.
- **Community** — for groups, cooperatives, and collectives (three or more
  members) building shared food systems, mutual aid, and reciprocal local
  economies.
- **Activator** — for facilitators and organizers who catalyze regeneration,
  connecting people, skills, and resources across the bioregion.
- **Living Lab** — for educational institutions and research sites integrating
  regenerative practice, citizen science, and hands-on ecological learning.

If more than one describes you, choose the one that best reflects how you will
use the support.

## What you will need

Every application asks for a few common things:

- Your contact details and location.
- A description of your work today and where you want to take it.
- The amount of support you are requesting, between 1,000 and 50,000 UBEC.
- A clear account of how the tokens will be used.
- A reflection on how your work embodies Ubuntu — *"I am because we are"* — the
  principle of reciprocity and shared flourishing at the centre of the commons.
- At least one reference who can speak to your work.

Each pathway also asks pathway-specific questions. Farmers describe their land,
products, and regenerative practices. Communities describe their membership,
governance, and mission. Activators describe their skills, networks, and a
year-one plan. Living Labs describe their site, curriculum, and the
environmental sensors they will use for monitoring.

## Submitting your application

1. Go to the [application page](/apply) and choose your pathway.
2. Complete the form. Fields marked with an asterisk are required.
3. Review your answers, agree to the terms and the shared Ubuntu values, and
   submit.
4. You will receive a **reference number** — keep it. It identifies your
   application in any follow-up.

## What happens next

Applications are received with a status of *pending* and reviewed by the
commons stewards. Review considers the strength of the regenerative commitment,
the clarity of the plan, and alignment with the Ubuntu values that guide the
protocol. You will be contacted using the details you provided.

## Questions

If you have questions before applying, use the [contact page](/about) to reach
the stewards. We would rather help you submit a strong application than have you
guess.
